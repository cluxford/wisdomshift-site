Why Wisdom Shift exists

Sell your wisdom, not your time.

Time does not scale. You only have so many hours, and every one of them is spoken for.

What you know can. Put it in a wisdom base, and it can be used in many more conversations than you could ever be part of.

How a conversation goes

Before, during and after.

Before

A short brief on where you left off with this person, and three things worth checking in on.

During

It listens. When something said fits one of your models, one prompt appears on your screen: a question, a quote or a model. When nothing fits, it stays quiet.

After

A summary, an email ready to send, and how you did against your own models.

If you have models you’d like others to use

Get your models used properly.

You know what works. The trouble is that it lives in your head and in your workshops, and everyone you teach drifts back to their old habits the moment you leave the room.

Put your models, questions, stories and quotes into your wisdom base. Then when other people give advice, Wisdom Shift brings up your model at the moment it fits, used the way you meant it, and your name stays on it.

You can earn from your models too. Invite other people to Wisdom Shift, and when they use your wisdom base as part of theirs, you earn a percentage of every session they run. You can only share what you wrote yourself, and your name stays on every item.

If you want to get better at applying the models

See how you really give advice, not how you think you do.

Nobody watches you work. You walk out of a conversation with a feeling about how it went, and that feeling is a poor guide. Wisdom Shift gives you something better to go on.

After every conversation

A short summary, an email to the other person that is ready to send, and three things to check in on next time.

Scored against your own models

Did you use the models you believe in? Did your questions go deep enough? Did they leave with something to do? You get an honest answer, with the moment in the conversation that shows why.

The gap between what you say and what you do

Across many conversations it shows the beliefs you hold, against how you actually behave. That is the thing you cannot see from inside one conversation.

Your progress, month by month

A clear line showing whether you are growing, and the one area worth working on next. If you need it, an ICF Core Competencies view is there too.

If you’ve been asked to use new models and need to learn and apply them

Learn it, then use it right, in the real conversation.

Your business has adopted a model. You went to the training. Then you were in a real conversation, the moment arrived, and you could not remember how it went.

Wisdom Shift closes that gap. It teaches you the model, checks you have understood it, then prompts you with it when the moment fits, and shows you afterwards whether you used it well.

It comes with standard models already built in, such as Transformative Learning, Mind Shifting and GROW, so you can start straight away.

Privacy and your data

Other people’s voices need careful handling.

A conversation belongs to everyone in it, so here is exactly what happens to it.

You tell them AI is listening

Before each conversation you confirm everyone on the call has been told, and you can record their consent. By default a visible indicator shows while AI is listening.

Leave their voices out

One setting keeps other people’s audio out of AI processing altogether.

Sorted by how sensitive it is

Scores and tags hold no personal information. Summaries are de-identified. Only raw transcripts are restricted, and you choose how long they are kept.

Export everything

Download your whole workspace as a ZIP, full transcripts included.

Delete it properly

Delete a workspace, one person, one learner or one session. You see exactly what will go first, and you keep a deletion certificate.

Plain about where it lives

Your data is stored in the United States. The agreement lists every provider that handles it, and where.
