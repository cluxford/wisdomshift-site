Wisdom Shift Cookie Notice

Effective date: 1 December 2026 · Last updated: 13 November 2026

The Wisdom Shift platform uses only the cookies and browser storage it needs to work.

We do not use:

  • advertising cookies;
  • analytics or tracking cookies;
  • cookies that follow you across other websites.

Because everything we use is strictly necessary, we do not ask for cookie consent. Turning these items off in your browser will stop the platform from working properly.

What we use

Name Type Purpose How long
Sign-in session Browser storage Keeps you signed in and renews your sign-in securely Until you sign out
ws_remember_until Browser storage Remembers your choice to stay signed in on this device Up to the period you choose
ws_asset Cookie (secure, HttpOnly) Lets your browser load your workspace's own images and files securely. It holds no personal information. 12 hours
ws_selected_mic_id Browser storage Remembers which microphone you chose Until cleared
ws_session_draft Browser storage Keeps a session you are setting up, so it is not lost if the page reloads Until the session starts
Display preferences Browser storage Remembers small choices such as date filters and dismissed reminders Until cleared

Payments

When you buy credits, Stripe, our payment provider, may set its own cookies. It uses them to process the payment securely and prevent fraud, and they are covered by Stripe's privacy policy.

Changes

If we ever add cookies that are not strictly necessary, we will update this notice and ask for your consent first.
